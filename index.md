# About Me
I am a professional Power Engineer working in Research & Development at **Schweitzer Engineering Laboratories (SEL)**. My career focuses on the safety, reliability, and automation of electric power systems. I specialize in designing and testing technical requirements for medium-voltage distribution feeders, protective relaying, and integrating advanced automation protocols.

Driven by a pursuit of lifelong learning, I am currently completing a **Master of Engineering (M.Engr.) in Electrical Engineering** at the **University of Idaho**, balancing my graduate studies with my full-time R&D engineering role. My technical and research interests sit at the intersection of Distributed Energy Resources (DERs), Power System Protection, Smart Grid reliability, and Supervisory Control systems.

I passed the Fundamentals of Engineering (FE) exam and am actively accumulating the specialized engineering experience required to attain full Professional Engineer (PE) licensure.
